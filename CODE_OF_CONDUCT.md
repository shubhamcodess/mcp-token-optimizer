# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free, welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to MCP.
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Explain the *why* in reviews.
- Credit others' work and ideas.
- Keep discussions on topic, and share data responsibly (never post secrets or other people's private information).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained pressure on maintainers
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, reviews) and when someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **private** channels: report the content with GitHub's "Report content" feature, or open a private [security advisory](SECURITY.md) and state that it is a conduct matter. Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers will respect the privacy of the reporter.

Maintainers who violate the Code of Conduct may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
